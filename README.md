# MindHaven

MindHaven is a personal mental-wellbeing platform that helps people record how they feel, understand changes in their daily life, practise simple wellness activities, and receive anonymous support from qualified professionals.

The platform brings mood, sleep, body signals, medication routines, personal context, and reflective exercises into one private wellbeing record. Over time, these records help users notice patterns and prepare clearer summaries that they can keep for themselves or share with someone they trust.

> MindHaven supports self-reflection and personal wellbeing tracking. It does not diagnose or treat mental-health conditions, replace professional care, or provide emergency services.

## What MindHaven does

### Daily check-in

The daily check-in gives users one guided place to record their mood, sleep, body signals, notes, and relevant context. A person can complete one check-in per day and return later to update that day's information instead of creating duplicate records.

The Today page summarizes the current day's progress, recent activity, weekly mood rhythm, and a suggested next step.

### Mood journal

The journal helps users describe and revisit their emotional experiences. A mood can be recorded using a 1–10 scale, an emoji, or a color scale.

Each entry can include a title, personal note, and context such as work, study, family, or weather. Guided templates support free reflection, difficult moments, gratitude, small wins, and trigger-response reflection. Users can search, filter, edit, and delete their entries.

### Body signals

The body-signal tracker helps users notice how emotional experiences appear physically. They can record sensations, affected body areas, intensity, emotions, and possible triggers.

Recent records and repeated observations are presented together so users can recognize patterns without treating them as a diagnosis.

### Sleep tracking

Users can record bedtime, wake time, sleep duration, and sleep quality. MindHaven calculates duration from the selected times when possible and summarizes recent sleep records to make changes easier to notice.

Sleep records can be updated or removed when information was entered incorrectly.

### Medication routines

The medication area helps users organize medication names, dosages, schedules, and preferred reminder times. Schedules can be edited, paused, resumed, or deleted.

For each day, users can record whether a scheduled medication was taken or skipped and review recent consistency. MindHaven records these decisions but does not prescribe medication or replace advice from a healthcare professional.

### Insights

The Insights page turns personal records into understandable summaries. Users can review mood trends, tracking coverage, context patterns, weekday rhythms, body-signal summaries, sleep patterns, and medication consistency.

When enough paired records exist, MindHaven can describe possible relationships such as sleep and mood or anxiety and mood. These results include the sample size and are presented as associations, not proof that one factor caused another.

### Reports and sharing

Users can create weekly, monthly, yearly, or custom-date reports and download them as PDF files. They can also export their complete account data in JSON format.

A report can be shared through an expiring link. The user chooses which sections to include, can revoke the link at any time, and can see basic access information. Anyone who receives an active link can view the selected information until it expires or is revoked.

### Wellness

The Wellness page provides guided breathing and meditation exercises for short, private pauses.

Breathing sessions include a visual breathing rhythm, written guidance, a timer, progress feedback, and pause, resume, and reset controls. Meditation sessions provide timed prompts and a complete written guide. Spoken prompts are optional when the user's browser supports them.

Wellness-session progress is not stored in the user's health records, and leaving the page ends the session.

### Care toolkit

The Care toolkit offers structured self-reflection exercises based on common cognitive-behavioural techniques. A user can describe a situation, record the first thought and emotion, review evidence for and against that thought, write a more balanced perspective, and compare emotional intensity before and after the exercise.

Thought records remain private and can be revisited, edited, or deleted. The toolkit also includes educational self-help practices and Bangladesh-focused support resources.

### Anonymous professional community

The Community page gives users a protected place to ask for support without displaying their account identity. Posts appear under a generated anonymous alias and can be public to signed-in users or private.

General members can read eligible posts but cannot give professional answers. Only a verified therapist can provide the initial professional response. After a response is accepted, the anonymous author and assigned therapist can continue a limited follow-up conversation.

If the author wants a different professional, they can request a therapist change. Another verified therapist can accept the request, while the existing conversation history remains available. Users can also report unsafe or inappropriate content.

### Professional therapist accounts

A therapist can register by selecting a specialization and optionally entering a workplace. Professional accounts begin in a pending state and cannot answer community posts until an administrator completes a separate verification process.

Once approved, a therapist can respond to an eligible unanswered post and continue conversations assigned to them. They cannot join conversations assigned to another therapist unless they accept an approved change request.

### Account and privacy controls

Users can create an account, sign in securely, update their profile, upload a profile picture, change their password, choose wellbeing goals, and manage interface preferences.

Personal tracker records remain connected to the authenticated account. Users can download their information and permanently delete their account and owned records.

### Administration

MindHaven includes a restricted administrator area for reviewing pending therapist accounts. An administrator can inspect the submitted professional details and approve or decline access to therapist-only community actions.

## Privacy and safety boundaries

- Community posts are anonymous to readers but remain connected to the author's account in the database for ownership and safety controls. This is pseudonymity rather than complete database anonymity.
- Professional accounts require approval before they can answer community posts.
- Personal insights show patterns and associations, not medical conclusions.
- Shared reports remain accessible to anyone who has an active share link.
- MindHaven does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or emergency intervention.
- Anyone in immediate danger should contact appropriate local emergency or professional support.
